# DCS ILARTS

The carrier's closed-circuit TV station for DCS World Supercarrier. You get the PLAT camera, both LSO station
screens and the deck cameras in their own window, from anywhere in the mission.

## Features

* **PLAT and LSO screens from anywhere:** in a cockpit, on another ship or in the F10 map.
* **Deck cameras:** an island camera follows each landing aircraft. Nine more deck positions have pan, tilt and zoom.
* **Automatic pass recording:** one MP4 per approach, named after the pilot, the case and the LSO grade.
* **Instant replay** with slow motion and frame stepping.
* **Black box:** a crash, ejection or lost aircraft near the carrier saves itself.
* **Up to four RTMP streams**, or a 1080p composite of up to four cameras.
* **Spout senders for OBS**, and **Windows 11 webcams** for Discord, Teams and Zoom.
* **REPAIR** after a DCS update: restores the hook and DCS's permissions.

## Requirements

DCS World 2.9.31.29434 with Supercarrier. Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit.

## ⚠ After you install: do not skip

1. Start `Mods\tech\ILARTS\bin\ILARTS.exe` once.
2. It says **"DCS ILARTS is not set up yet"**: click **Yes**. This adds the hook's two permissions to
   `Saved Games\DCS\Config\autoexec.cfg`. **Without it, ILARTS does not work in single player or when you host.**
3. Load a mission with a carrier. ILARTS does not open when DCS starts: it opens about 10 seconds after the mission has loaded.

**Before you uninstall:** in ILARTS, click SETTINGS > Installation > DCS permissions > **REMOVE**.

Read `Mods\tech\ILARTS\README.txt` for the full reference.
